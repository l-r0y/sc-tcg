sc-tcg is a trading card game inspired by **StarCraft** (1, 2 & Brood War) and **Magic: The Gathering**. This section is the living documentation of the project: what the game is, how it plays, and why design decisions are made the way they are. Dated reflections and announcements live in the [Blog](../posts/); this wiki holds the stable reference material.

## How the documentation is structured

* A **section** (Overview, Rules, Design) has an overview page that lists all of its articles in reading order.
* An **article** focuses on a single topic. Its footer always links to the previous and next article of the section, plus back to the section overview.
* The **sidebar** is available on every documentation page for direct access to any article; new topics can get their own page as the design evolves.

> Much of this documentation is still being written — pages in progress are marked *Draft*. For background on the project and its history, see [History & Influences](overview/history.html).
