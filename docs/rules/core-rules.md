This page is the skeleton of the rulebook: game setup, turn structure and victory conditions. The individual card types are documented in [Card Types](card-types.html).

> *Draft — the rules below define the frame of a game; concrete numbers (deck size, resource amounts) will be filled in as they are finalized.*

## Game setup

1. Each player builds a deck from their collection (size: to be finalized — see [Design Principles](../design/design-principles.html)).
2. Players take alternate turns starting with one of them (coin flip / first-player rule).
3. On your turn you spend resources to play cards and activate them, following the order described below.

## Turn structure

A turn is divided into phases:

* **Start phase** — refresh or gain resources, upkeep effects.
* **Main phase** — play cards from hand onto the field.
* **Action phase** — command units and abilities already in play.
* **End phase** — cleanup, pass the game to your opponent.

## Victory conditions

A game ends when one of these happens:

* A player fulfills their faction's win condition (to be defined per faction).
* A player has no way to continue playing (decked out / unable to take actions).

Ties and draw rules will be documented here once settled.

## Where this goes next

The frame above is stable; the details live elsewhere: card types in [Card Types](card-types.html), and the *why* behind phase structure and resources in [Design Principles](../design/design-principles.html).
