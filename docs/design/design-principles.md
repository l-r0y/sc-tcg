This page collects the working design goals of sc-tcg. They are deliberately high-level: they should let any future decision (a new card type, a rule change, an art direction) be checked against *why* the game exists in the first place.

> *Draft — these pillars are starting points to refine, not final doctrine.*

## Working pillars

1. **Faction identity above all.** Terran, Zerg and Protoss decks must feel like playing different games (their StarCraft counterparts do). A card that could belong to any faction is a suspect card.
2. **Readable at the table.** Card text should be parseable in seconds — Magic's rules-language density is useful but a ceiling, not a target.
3. **Tension over tempo.** The StarCraft fantasy lives on decisions with real cost (commit or hold back). Design favors moments of choice over automatic sequences.

## Constraints & open questions

| Question | Current answer | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How big is a deck? | Not finalized yet | Open |
| What is the resource? | A single shared resource type for now | Open |
| How many factions ship in v1? | The three classics (Terran / Zerg / Protoss) | Working assumption |
