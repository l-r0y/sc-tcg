sc-tcg is a **trading card game (TCG)** inspired by two very different sources: the faction-driven unit warfare of **StarCraft** (1, 2 and Brood War), and the card-mechanics depth of **Magic: The Gathering**.

## What you play

* Two players build decks of cards from their own collection.
* In a turn-based game, each player commands units and abilities on the field, spending resources to bring them into play and reacting to the opponent's moves — the strategic feel of a StarCraft match, expressed as a card game.
* Games end when one player achieves the victory condition (see [Core Rules](../rules/core-rules.html#victory-conditions)).

## What makes sc-tcg different

* **Faction identity first.** The three classic StarCraft factions — Terran, Zerg and Protoss — drive distinct card pools and play styles, just as they drove distinct strategies in the original games.
* **MTG-style card text.** Cards have costs, types and written abilities that interact with each other, so deck building is a real design exercise (see [Card Types](../rules/card-types.html)).
